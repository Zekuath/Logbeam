Your own secret patterns

Regular expressions for keys and tokens Logbeam doesn't know, such as an internal token format. The whole match is treated as the secret, or its first group if it has one: acme_[A-Za-z0-9]{32}, X-Internal-Key: (\S+). They are used by the log viewer, Compare and "Mask secrets".

Your own level words

        

          Words your logs use for a level that Logbeam doesn't know, such as
          ALERT, CRIT or AUDIT.
          A word is matched as written where a level usually is
          (ALERT disk full, [ALERT]), and in any case after
          level=
          or in a JSON level field.
